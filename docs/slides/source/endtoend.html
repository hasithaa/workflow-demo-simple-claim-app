One claim, end to end

1 · Ann

Submits the claim

The Claims API starts processClaim, which records the claim and waits.

BILL_REQUIRED

2 · Ann

Uploads the bill

The bill reaches the waiting workflow as an event, and it moves on.

IN_REVIEW

3 · Workflow

Asks a manager

It creates an approval task for the MANAGER role. Jane sees it in Approvals.

Task: approveClaim

4 · Jane

Approves

The workflow records the decision and her comment. Ann's portal shows it.

APPROVED

The same story as the demo, seen from the workflow's side. Two of the four steps are people, two are the workflow. Between step 1 and step 2 the workflow simply waits, for seconds or for weeks: nothing polls and nothing is lost if the integration restarts.
